# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want Hydroloop to be a welcoming, respectful project for contributors of all experience levels.

Examples of positive behavior:

- being respectful and constructive
- assuming good intent
- giving actionable feedback
- helping others learn

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment or personal attacks
- discriminatory language
- doxxing or intimidation
- bad-faith disruption of the project

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that harm the health of the project community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness inappropriate behavior, contact the maintainer privately with relevant context.
